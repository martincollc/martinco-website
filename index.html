Who you work with

Founder-led. Field-tested.

Martin & Company is the consulting practice of Kyle J. Martin — sixteen-plus years across the rail and transit industry, from signal maintainer and electronic technician to construction manager and senior project director.

Kyle specializes in delivering complex infrastructure within live-service environments: electrification, station upgrades, signal systems, bus rapid transit, and multimodal programs. His work is defined by disciplined outage planning, rigorous constructability review, clear stakeholder coordination, and an uncompromising commitment to safety and regulatory compliance.

The firm was founded to bring that same owner’s-representative rigor directly to transit agencies, contractors, and infrastructure owners — senior attention on every program, start to finish.

Now based in South Florida and active within FDOT District 4, Kyle brings program experience spanning New England, California, Maryland, and South Florida to every engagement — a national perspective applied to local delivery.
