Hump Day Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Hump Day has no accounts and we never see your name, email or location. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads. You can watch ads only when you choose to.

This policy explains how Hump Day (the "app"), published by Martin & Company Consulting LLC under the name MartinCo Games ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for the app and we do not collect personal information. There is no sign-up, login or chat.

  • Game progress and settings (levels completed, stars, coins, coal, sound, music and language choices) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. We cannot see them.
  • Age answer. On first launch the app asks for your birth year so it can choose the right kind of ads. This answer is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show optional reward videos, for example to get more steam, more coal, a hint or a level skip. To deliver, measure and limit fraud on these ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and operating system,
  • ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or watched.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent: in regions that require it, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice later from the app's Options screen.
  • Players under 13: if the age answer shows the player is under 13, the app asks Google to treat ad requests as child-directed and to show only non-personalized ads.
  • Opting out: you can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your Android settings under Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Children

Hump Day is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. See "Players under 13" above for how ads are handled for younger players.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we have none to share. The only third party that receives device information through the app is Google, for advertising, as described above.

Security and retention

Because your progress is stored only on your device, it stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Change your ad consent choice from Options in the app, where available.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Changes

If we change how the app handles information, for example by adding purchases, we will update this page and the effective date above.
