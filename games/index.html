Hump Day

A shunting puzzle played with wooden toy trains on a kid's bedroom floor.

Read the work order, then pull, shove and drop cars between short sidings to build the train in exactly the right order. The engine only pulls a few cars at a time, so every move counts. Beat each level at par for three stars.

  • 30 levelsEvery one checked by a solver
  • 19 languagesDetected from your phone

Moving Day

You and a driver have to empty a hoarder's mansion, one box at a time.

Start with a beat-up pickup and get paid by the box. Toss boxes out the window if you dare (damages come out of your pay), crack the safe instead of hauling it, and maybe pocket something on the way out. Just don't get caught.

About the studio

MartinCo Games is the game studio of Martin & Company Consulting LLC, based in South Florida. Our day job is railroad and transit construction, which is why trains keep showing up in our games (dogs and flying cats, too).

We keep our games honest: no pay-to-win, ads you can switch off, and puzzles that are fair.
